Returns & Refunds Policy

Clear, fair, and transparent return guidelines for a risk-free shopping experience.

1. 7-Day Hassle-Free Returns Window

Most items purchased on ShopKart are eligible for return or replacement within 7 days of verified delivery. To qualify, products must be unused, in original condition, and include all original tags, accessories, manuals, and packaging materials.

2. Eligible Return Reasons

  • Product arrived damaged, defective, or physically compromised during transit.
  • Item received differs in color, specifications, or model from what was ordered.
  • Missing accessories, parts, or promotional bundles included in the listing.
  • Size or fit issue on eligible apparel and footwear items.

3. Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel their order directly online while the order is in Placed or Confirmed & Packed status before warehouse dispatch. Once cancelled, any payments made are automatically queued for refund, and reserved inventory is immediately returned to stock.

5. How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return or exchange, visit your My Orders hub, or contact our support team through the Contact Us portal with your Order ID and photos of the item.
