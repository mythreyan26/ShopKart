Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about orders, shipping, returns, and account security.

You can track your order at any time by heading to your My Orders page and clicking "Track Order & Details". Every order includes an interactive 4-step progress stepper (Placed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered) along with live courier tracking numbers and estimated arrival dates.
Yes! You can cancel any order directly from the order details page as long as its status is still Order Placed or Confirmed & Packed. Once cancelled, reserved product inventory is instantly restored to the catalog. If an item has already been dispatched/shipped, you can initiate a return upon delivery.
ShopKart features a dynamic coupon engine. In your Cart or Checkout screen, enter any active coupon code (such as WELCOME10 or SAVE20) and click "Apply". Discounts are calculated either as a percentage off with a maximum cap, or a flat rupee amount off.
Orders with a subtotal of ₹499 or higher qualify for FREE standard delivery anywhere in India! For orders below ₹499, a nominal delivery fee of ₹40 applies. Your cart automatically shows a live progress bar tracking how much more you need to add for free shipping.
We support Cash on Delivery (COD), Instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay). All transactions are encrypted with industry-standard protocol safeguards.
Yes! ShopKart is a certified Progressive Web App (PWA). When visiting on Chrome or Safari mobile, tap the "Install App" banner or browser options to add ShopKart to your home screen. It operates in full-screen standalone mode with offline page caching. Additionally, a dedicated Expo / React Native mobile app codebase is located in mobile_app/.
